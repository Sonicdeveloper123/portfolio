About the madrasa

Careful recitation, sound understanding and good character.

Our story

We began in a single room beside the mosque with twelve students. Today the madrasa serves families across the neighborhood, and the approach is the same: teach the Quran well, and teach students to live by it.

Every class is capped at fifteen students. Teachers hold ijazah in recitation, and parents receive a progress report each term.

  • Classes of 15 or fewerEnough attention for every student to be heard, every day.
  • Boys' and girls' sectionsSeparate classrooms with qualified male and female teachers.
  • Open to all levelsFrom first letters to advanced tajweed and Arabic grammar.

What we value

Adab first

Respect for teachers, parents and one another comes before any lesson.

Patience with beginners

No student is rushed. Mistakes are how recitation improves.

Partnership with parents

Regular meetings so learning continues at home.
