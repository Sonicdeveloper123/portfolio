Programs

Choose the path that fits the student's age and goals.

Nazra and Tajweed

Ages 5 and up

Reading the Quran fluently, starting from the Arabic letters. Students learn tajweed step by step and recite aloud every lesson.

Hifz

Ages 8 and up, full time

Full memorization of the Quran with daily revision (muraja'ah) and monthly recitation reviews. Each student follows an individual pace.

Islamic Studies

Ages 7 to 16, after school

Aqidah, fiqh of daily worship, seerah, duas and Islamic manners, taught in plain language with stories and discussion.

Arabic Language

Ages 10 and up

Grammar, vocabulary and reading, so students can understand what they recite.

Adult Evening Classes

Weeknights, 7:30 to 9:00 pm

Quran reading for beginners, tajweed correction and weekly study circles for adults.
